A translation is missing where the satellite of a language has no value for a key of the English file, or a null or empty value. A key whose English holds no letter is not counted, and neither is a key that the language's translator judged to need no translation ([`not-needed/`](not-needed/)). The keys of each language are in [`missing/<language>.tsv`](missing/).

- **Not translated**: ca, he, id, ku, vec, zh-HK translate almost nothing and are not to be translated (@Pauli, Nov 16). Their rows show what they lack; the row "All that are translated" leaves them out.
- **Done** is the share of the 1631 keys that the language translates or does not need.
- **Not needed** counts the keys that the language's translator judged to need no translation, in `not-needed/<language>.tsv`. They are not in **Missing**.
- **By a model** counts the translations whose entry names in its comment the language model that made it: `translator=<model id>; effort=<effort setting>; date=<date>` (README.md).
- **In the repo** counts the missing translations that a satellite in `src/PurplePen` holds. Upstream's TranslateTool writes the satellites of PurplePenCore's resources there. PurplePen.csproj removes them from its build, and PurplePenCore has no copy, so the application does not show them.
- **Fork's own** counts the missing translations of keys that `origin/base` does not have. They are translated on the branch that adds the key.
