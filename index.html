NotPurplePen · SVG target

SVG Logbook

NotPurplePen, a fork of the orienteering course-setting program Purple Pen, is getting a graphics target that writes maps and courses as SVG. After each step of the work, this log shows the test drawings the target can draw, each three ways: drawn from its SVG by your browser, drawn by the Skia target as the reference, and drawn from the SVG by Chromium in the test run.
